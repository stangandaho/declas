Built for the people who empty the camera traps

Declas is a free, open-source desktop application that uses AI to detect wildlife and identify species in camera trap images and videos, and turns the results into flat, analysis-ready data.

Why Declas exists

A single camera trap survey can produce hundreds of thousands of images, most of them empty. Sorting them by hand takes months that field teams rarely have. Cloud platforms help, but they need fast internet, accounts and uploads of sensitive location data, which is a real barrier for many projects in Africa and other places with limited connectivity.

Declas brings the same AI models to your own laptop. Install it once, download a model once, and process your surveys offline, wherever you are. The output is a plain CSV you can analyse in R, Python or a spreadsheet, with no lock-in.

Principles

  • Your images and their GPS positions never leave your computer.
  • MIT-licensed code, a public model registry, and results in plain JSON and CSV.
  • Any team can publish a model for its region as an extension.
  • Every species, count and box can be corrected before export.
  • Time in radians, per-individual distances and tag columns designed for activity and density analyses.

Declas is developed by Stanislas Gandaho with contributions from the community. It builds on the work of the model authors credited in the model list, including Microsoft AI for Good (MegaDetector), the DeepFaune initiative, Addax Data Science and the San Diego Zoo Wildlife Alliance.

Choose Declas if…

you work offline or with limited bandwidth, need to keep locations private, want per-animal distances for distance sampling, or need typed custom fields alongside AI results on Windows, macOS or Linux.

Consider the others if…

you need a shared online database for a large multi-partner programme (Wildlife Insights, TrapTagger), individual identification (TrapTagger), or a mature manual-annotation workflow on Windows (Timelapse, which pairs well with AddaxAI).
