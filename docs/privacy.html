Privacy notice

Short version: the Declas app collects nothing. This website collects only what you give us, to tell you about updates and to answer your messages.

The Declas application

Declas runs on your computer. Your images, videos, results and their locations are never sent anywhere. The app only connects to the internet to download the model registry and the model weights you choose.

Download form

The first time you download Declas from a computer, we ask for your first name, last name, email address and country. We use them only to email you when a new version of Declas is released, and to know roughly where Declas is used.

To avoid asking you again, we store a random identifier in your browser and a one-way hash of your IP address. We never store your IP address itself. Because people on the same network can share an IP address, another person on your network may not be asked to fill in the form.

Download counter

Each download is counted with its operating system, version and date, linked to the same anonymous identifiers. The total shown on the site includes downloads made from GitHub and Zenodo before this site existed.

Contact form

Messages you send are stored so we can reply, and are deleted when no longer needed.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share your data.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
  • We do not send anything other than Declas news.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or stop receiving update emails, at any time by writing to stangandaho@gmail.com.

Data is stored with our database provider, Supabase. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, and the video tutorial is embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. Last updated: October 2026.
